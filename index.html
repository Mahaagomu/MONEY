Learn & Protect Yourself

Essential Safety Tips

Simple steps to protect your money and identity from online fraud in India.

🔗

Check the URL Carefully

Scammers copy brand names with slight changes: amaz0n, flipkarrt, paytm-offer.xyz. Always read the full domain before clicking.

URL Safety
🔒

HTTPS Is Not Enough

HTTPS only means data is encrypted — it does NOT mean the site is safe. Scam sites can also have HTTPS. Always check the domain name too.

Encryption
🚫

Never Share OTP / CVV / PIN

No real bank, government, or company will EVER ask for these. Anyone asking — on call, SMS, or chat — is a scammer.

Critical Rule
📲

UPI Collect = You Pay

Scammers send a "collect" request disguised as a payment. Approving it deducts money FROM you. Never approve UPI requests from strangers.

UPI Fraud
🛒

Fake Shopping Websites

Unbelievably low prices, no return policy, no physical address — red flags. Always use verified platforms: Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Meesho.

Shopping
📧

Phishing SMS & Email

Don't click links in unexpected messages. Open your browser and type the official website address directly instead of clicking.

Phishing
📞

Fake Customer Care Numbers

Top Google results for "bank customer care" can be fake numbers. Only trust numbers printed on your debit/credit card or the bank's official app.

Phone Fraud
🏦

Enable Bank Alerts

Turn on SMS/push notifications for every transaction. A few seconds of awareness can prevent large losses — report suspicious charges immediately.

Banking

Stay Updated

Latest Scam Alerts

Current fraud trends in India and worldwide. Updated regularly by our team.

India · May 2025

Fake IPL Ticket Websites

Fraudulent sites offering discounted match tickets steal payment details. Buy only from official BCCI or BookMyShow portals.

🔴 High Risk
India · Apr 2025

Aadhaar Update Phishing SMS

SMS messages asking you to click a link to "update Aadhaar" or lose mobile service. UIDAI never sends such messages — delete immediately.

🔴 High Risk
India · Apr 2025

Electricity Bill Disconnection Scam

WhatsApp messages about power disconnection with a fake helpline. Calling leads to AnyDesk remote access fraud and account takeover.

🟡 Medium Risk
Global · Mar 2025

AI Voice Cloning Calls

Scammers clone voices of relatives using AI and call victims claiming emergencies. Verify by calling back on a known saved number.

🟡 Medium Risk
India · Mar 2025

Fake WhatsApp Part-Time Jobs

Groups offering ₹500–₹2000 per task for "liking videos." After small initial payments, victims are asked to invest larger amounts and cheated.

🔴 High Risk
India · Feb 2025

Fake Amazon/Flipkart Refund Calls

Callers pretend to be customer care offering refunds, then request screen sharing to steal banking credentials. Always call official numbers only.
